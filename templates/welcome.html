Workflow

From raw video to ready-to-post

1 · Upload your video

MP4, MOV, WEBM from your phone or desktop — or paste a public link.

2 · Add a reference

Paste a TikTok / Reel / Short you love. Clipy retrieves it and analyzes its edit style.

3 · AI builds the plan

Best moments, hooks, captions, zooms and pacing — mapped onto your video.

4 · Render & publish

Real FFmpeg rendering, then schedule posts to TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

New

"Use a video as inspiration"

The AI doesn't just read a URL — Clipy actually retrieves the public reference video, samples its frames, listens to its audio, and produces a structured editing profile:

  • Editing style, hook structure & opening sequence
  • Shot pacing, transitions and camera movement
  • Caption style, position and animation
  • Zoom effects, color grade and sound cues
  • Ending style and storytelling rhythm

Public videos only. If a platform refuses access, upload the reference file instead — we never bypass access controls.
