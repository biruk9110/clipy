How editing works

Two ways to make clips — use both together for best results.

Upload your video

MP4, MOV or WEBM. Clipy transcribes it with Whisper, then Gemini finds the strongest moments — or you can guide it with a reference video.

Add a reference video · optional

Paste a public TikTok / Reel / Short URL you like the style of (or upload the file). The AI studies its frames and audio — cuts, captions, zooms, pacing — and applies that editing style to your video. We recreate the approach, never the content.
